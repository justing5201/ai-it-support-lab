# Mobile phone setup intake

## Purpose

Fictional Alder Works training procedure. Use for a company mobile phone setup or work email enrollment request.

## Initial Information to Collect

1. Is the device company owned or personal?
2. Which device model and application need setup?
3. Is enrollment approved and is an approved guide available?

## User-Safe Actions

1. Record the phone model and setup error.
2. Use only the approved enrollment guide supplied by IT.
3. Do not share passwords, recovery codes or MFA codes in a ticket.

## Technician Actions

1. Verify ownership, eligibility and enrollment approval.
2. Have the authorized device administrator perform enrollment or policy changes.
3. Verify access with the user after approved setup; record the observed result.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate operational outages, unknown scope, missing approvals, repeated faults or any action outside technician authority. Security reports always require designated security review. Building access and camera changes require their designated owner.

## Resolution Criteria

Keep the ticket open until an authorized person records an observed successful test. Suggested checks are not completed actions.
