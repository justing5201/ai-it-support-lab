# New hire laptop and access intake

## Purpose

Fictional Alder Works training procedure. Use for new employee setup, imaging and access requests. All access needs approved authorization.

## Initial Information to Collect

1. What are the start date, location, manager and device assignment?
2. Which applications and access are requested?
3. Has the authorized approver approved each access request?

## User-Safe Actions

1. Ask the requester to provide the start date and manager contact.
2. Collect required applications and device needs without requesting passwords.

## Technician Actions

1. Verify the request and approvals through the approved internal process.
2. Assign and record a lab asset and setup checklist.
3. Have the authorized endpoint team perform imaging and approved software installation.
4. Have the identity owner provision approved access; verify delivery with the requester.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate operational outages, unknown scope, missing approvals, repeated faults or any action outside technician authority. Security reports always require designated security review. Building access and camera changes require their designated owner.

## Resolution Criteria

Keep the ticket open until an authorized person records an observed successful test. Suggested checks are not completed actions.
