# Conference room display and audio

## Purpose

Fictional Alder Works training procedure. Use for missing conference room display, microphone or speaker audio.

## Initial Information to Collect

1. Which room, laptop and meeting application are affected?
2. Is the problem audio, video or both?
3. Does another laptop show the same symptom?
4. When is the meeting and is an alternative room available?

## User-Safe Actions

1. Check the selected meeting speaker, microphone and display.
2. Check mute and visible cable connections without changing installed equipment.
3. Ask whether another laptop has the same problem.
4. Record the error and whether an alternative room is available.

## Technician Actions

1. Compare the room configuration with the documented working setup.
2. Gather device and application versions for the AV owner.
3. Schedule approved changes outside the meeting where possible.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate operational outages, unknown scope, missing approvals, repeated faults or any action outside technician authority. Security reports always require designated security review. Building access and camera changes require their designated owner.

## Resolution Criteria

Keep the ticket open until an authorized person records an observed successful test. Suggested checks are not completed actions.
